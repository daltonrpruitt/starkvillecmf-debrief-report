### Things We Could Not Control

#### This Specific Issue

Data centers are currently in the national spotlight due to things like "artificial intelligence".
Cryptocurrency as well is a hot topic for some groups. 

In addition, this proposed facility would have been located pretty centrally to the city (see [here](https://starkvillecmf.org/#/archive/concerns?id=location-proximity-to-vulnerable-populations) for pictures[^3]), meaning it potentially would impact many different groups, including businesses, animals, and businesses.

I believe that this basically led to a kind of "perfect storm" of opposition to the project, including:
- those concerned with resource usage locally and impacts to utilities bills,
- those that oppose crypto currency on a moral basis, 
- those concerned about potential noise impacts to animals and wildlife, 
- those that are concerned about the power usage from an environmental standpoint (emissions from power generation), 
- people concerned with the obfuscated ownership structure of the project, and 
- those that are concerned about the usage of capacity of the substation for cryptocurrency instead of other industries that provide services or produce physical goods.

These parties spanned political parties, religions, and other dimensions we are so typically divided. 
This broad support for our cause (opposition to the project) led to overwhelming numbers at the Planning and Zoning Commission. 
Without the organization of people and speeches beforehand, I would bet that there still would have been more people present to oppose the project than the number that are normally present at the Planning and Zoning Commission or Board of Aldermen meetings.

It may not have been as overwhelming of a response if the organizing had not have occurred, but I wanted to point out how this specific issue itself was such an almost toxic topic in and of itself that the community would have still done something else to organize before the Board of Aldermen meeting at least.


## Extensive research and argument vetting

A large part of our effort surrounding this issue was research on this and similar facilities. 
We had subgroups working on researching utilties usage (electricity and water), pollution (noise, heat, and air), and company identity and a group discussing speechwriting.
All of these were on top of the main group where general information and discussions were had. 

Several types of information were shared:
- official documents, 
- news articles, 
- our own written words, 
- and other sources/repositories of information. 




### My Background and Positioning Coming into this Situation

I am risk averse, commitment avoidant[^4], and anti-traditionalist[^5]. 
In these aspects, I likely should have been set up to not care about this problem, or at least not want to devote a lot of time to it. 

However, I care a lot about people, animals, and the environment in which we live. 
This care coupled with the risk-averse nature means that the possibility of harm coming to my fellow community members was greatly alarming to me. 
I honsetly did not know enough at the time to weigh in on the discussion, but I started to look into it and ask questions where possible. 

Within the last two years, I have developed an appreciation for other people and the benefits (social, emotional, spiritual, and sometimes financial) that having a lot of friends and acquaintances can have[^6]. 
I started doing my best to get into places where I could interact with others, including volunteer work, my own business helping people directly, and membership in a business networking group[^7] and several local organizations trying to help our community.
I've made more friends and acquaintances in the past 2 years than I had in the previous 5 or so (which isn't saying much due to the COVID years...).
I like talking to people and helping them work through their problems, even if I may not have a direct solution. 
I now understand that my being so open to conversation and approaching people allowed me to reach out to others that I saw as like-minded and similarly caring that would help us figure out what to do. 
We formed many layers of groups and connections between our individual circles of influence (friends, family, religious and non-religious institutions, etc.) that led to where we ultimately ended up in this work. 
We may have gotten there eventually, but I beleive that my training to lower the "activation energy of social interaction"[^8] allowed us to make some core connections early on in the effort that gave us ample time to grow our group and work. 

Ideally, start early every time, but growing fast in limiited time is also a way to get there.
